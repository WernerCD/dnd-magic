Tool Changes

D&D Spell Planner

Pick, rank and share spell lists for 2014 and 2024 D&D 5e

Changes

What has changed on the Spell Planner, newest first. Back to the Spell Planner.

November 19, 2026

  • Core books first. The Sources menu now lists the core rules (the Player's Handbook and the SRD spells in it) first and ticked, then official supplements such as Xanathar's and Tasha's, settings and adventures, and partner books such as Arcana Unleashed, all still available but unticked. Core books goes back to that default, and changing class or rules keeps it.
  • Spells from imported characters. When a character imported on the Toolbox Characters page (from D&D Beyond, Foundry VTT or Fight Club 5) brings spells, the Spell Planner offers to make a new list from them, with prepared spells marked. Spell names it can't find are listed, and your existing lists are never changed. Spells marked known-but-not-prepared are left out (and named) except for a Wizard's spellbook, spells from a species, feat or item are listed but not counted against a class, and DiceCloud imports are supported too.
  • Toolbox menus. The top bar now has centred drop-down menus (Characters, Build, Play, Run) to jump to any other D&D Toolbox page; on a phone they sit under one Tools button.

November 18, 2026

  • Motion and charts. Spell cards fade in as you scroll and catch a soft light under the pointer, and notices and the spell dialog slide in. Your list panel gains two small charts: your spells by level and your own ratings, each with a "Show as table" view and arrow-key reading. With "reduce motion" on, everything shows at once.
  • Character sheet summary. A character's main spell list is now shown on the D&D Toolbox Character Sheet and Party pages: each spell with its level, casting time, range, concentration and ritual tags, whether it is prepared or always prepared, its class, and your rating. It updates as you change the list.

November 15, 2026

  • Your own spell ratings. Give any spell 1 to 5 stars from its row in your list or from its detail view (click a star, or use the arrow keys; click the same star again to clear it). Ratings are kept with each list, show on the spell cards, and travel in share links, character share links and exports. Sort by My rating, or pick ratings in the new My rating filter menu (tick 5 and 4 for "4 stars or more").
  • Character share links carry the spell list. When you share a character from the header, its spell list (with your ratings and notes) goes with it. Whoever accepts the link gets the list linked to that character; if they already have a different list for it, the shared one is added beside theirs as "(shared)" instead of replacing it.

November 14, 2026

  • Filter menus with tick boxes. Level, School, Casting time, Components, Concentration and ritual, Damage, and Save or attack are now drop-down menus where you can tick several options. Each option shows how many spells it would give with your other filters, and options with none are dimmed. The new Components menu finds spells with no verbal, somatic, material or costly material component. Source counts follow your filters too. Old filter links still work.
  • Safer character syncing. A character's list now follows only changes to its rules, classes, levels, subclasses or scores. If you changed the classes in the list yourself, you are asked whether to use the character's classes or keep yours. A deleted character leaves its list in place, an unknown character link says so, Escape closes the filter menus, and lists changed in another tab are picked up.
  • Fixes. A character with four or more classes now counts every class level (up to four class rows; extra classes join the last row). Subclass spells you also added to your list, and a Warlock's Mystic Arcanum, show once, marked, and no longer count against your known or prepared spells. Eight 2024 spells no longer appear twice with "Spells from older books" on. Unknown filter values in a link are ignored with a short notice, a list too long for a share link says so, and opening a share link no longer removes a character's empty list.

November 13, 2026

  • Shared characters. Pick a character in the header (made in the Multiclass Planner or on any D&D Toolbox site) and the Spell Planner opens a spell list for it with its rules, classes, levels, subclasses and spellcasting modifiers filled in. When the character levels up on another site, its list follows; your spells, ranks, tiers and notes stay as they are. A list can also be saved as a new shared character from the list menu, or unlinked.
  • Classes without spells by name. Levels in a class that has no spellcasting now show as that class (Fighter, Monk, Barbarian, Rogue) instead of "Other class", both for a shared character and when you add one by hand.
  • Multiclassing. Add up to three classes with + Add a class (a class without spellcasting can count toward your character level). The rules card adds up your caster level for the shared spell slots (2014 and 2024 rounding), lists each class's cantrips, known or prepared spells and the highest level it can learn, and keeps Pact Magic separate. Each class's subclass spells (a Paladin oath, a Cleric domain...) show as always prepared at that class's level. In your list, pick which class each spell counts for; a spell that is not on any of your class lists counts as "other". Share links carry all your classes.
  • Launch. Pick 2014 or 2024 rules, a class, subclass and level to see the class spell list, your spell slots, cantrips and known or prepared spells, with subclass spells marked. Every SRD 5.1 and 5.2 spell has its full text; spells from other official books are listed with their facts. Build your own spell lists: add spells, rank them by dragging or with the arrow buttons, give them a tier and a note, keep several lists, share one with a link, export and import a backup, or print a spell sheet.
