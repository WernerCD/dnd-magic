    My ratings

    Add spells with + on a card. Drag the handle, or use the arrow buttons, to rank spells within a level. Pick a tier and add a note to remember why. Your lists are saved in this browser; use Copy share link or Export in the … menu to keep a copy elsewhere.

    About

    Choose the rules, class, subclass and level to see that class's spell list, its spell slots and how many cantrips and spells you know or prepare. Spells your subclass always has prepared, or adds to your list, are marked. Add spells to your own lists, rank them, give them a tier and a note, and keep as many lists as you like. Everything stays in your browser.

It was retrieved through the Open5e API. Spells and subclasses from other books are listed with facts only (level, school, casting time, range, components, duration, classes, book and page); their rules text is in the book named. Those facts, the class tables and the subclass spell lists were compiled from the community 5etools dataset. Unofficial fan content; not affiliated with or endorsed by Wizards of the Coast.
